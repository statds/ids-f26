# CSAS progress examples

These are designed acceptance cases, not captured agent test results.
External abstract deadline: December 1, 2026. No cutoff hour is assumed.
Initial plan: outline Nov 17–21, draft Nov 22–26, revise Nov 27–30.

- Early completion: user confirms on Nov 20 that outline is done. On Nov 21,
  if time is available, recommend starting the draft. Preserve the actual
  completion date and update remaining phase dates from user estimates.
- Delay: on Nov 22 the outline still needs one workday. If Nov 22 is available,
  propose finishing then and starting the draft Nov 23. Obtain remaining
  drafting/revision effort before assigning their end dates. Dec 1 stays fixed.
- No reply: status is unknown. Say “If outline is done, draft next; otherwise
  confirm what remains.” Do not mark complete or invent a delay.
- Capacity conflict: confirmed remaining work cannot fit by Dec 1. Report the
  shortfall and ask what can change. Never move the organizer's deadline.

Suggested persisted fields: task_id, phase_id, status, confirmed_at,
confirmation_source, actual_finish, remaining_work, available_sessions,
hard_deadline, proposed_start, proposed_end, change_reason.
